The word we work by.

Not values on a wall. Five things a client can hold us to, written the way we'd say them across a bakkie bonnet.

  1. OneEvery pilot flies under the Otto name.

    No handing your farm over to a subcontractor we haven't vetted and approved to fly under the Otto family name.

  2. TwoWe stop when the wind says stop.

    Drift onto a neighbour's vines is somebody's whole season. If conditions turn we stand down and come back, even when standing down costs us the day.

  3. ThreeSupply your own materials, or let us source them.

    Our pilots are POC certified and can fly your own mixtures, or we can source and supply for each job.

  4. FourWe'll tell you when we're the wrong people.

    Some jobs belong to a ground crew, a contractor, or nobody at all. We'd rather lose the quote than take money for work a drone shouldn't be doing.

  5. FiveEvery report leaves here signed.

    A name at the bottom of the page, by hand. If something is wrong with it, you know exactly whose phone to ring.

Every flight log, coverage map and inspection report that leaves this business carries a signature. It is the oldest accountability tool there is, and it still works.
